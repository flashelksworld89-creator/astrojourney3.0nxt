# AstroWalk 3.7.6 Neuro-Experiential Prose Model

AstroWalk uses neuroscience only as a **narration framework**. It does not claim that planets, transits, houses, nakshatras, or other astrological factors activate, diagnose, damage, or alter specific brain regions or cranial nerves.

## Why this model exists

The prediction engine already builds Vedic evidence and concrete event candidates. Version 3.7.6 adds a disciplined human-experience sequence so the final prose describes how an event might actually be encountered:

1. **Sensory entry** — what may first be seen, heard, physically noticed, smelled, tasted, or spatially detected.
2. **Recognition** — what the person may infer or realize from the cue.
3. **Decision** — what practical choice may follow.
4. **Speech / motor response** — what the person may say or physically do next.

## Anatomy used as the language framework

- Visual input: optic system / occipital processing.
- Auditory and balance input: vestibulocochlear system / temporal and spatial processing.
- Facial sensation and oral handling: trigeminal and related mixed cranial-nerve functions.
- Facial expression, taste, voice, swallowing, head movement and tongue articulation: relevant cranial-nerve motor/sensory functions.
- Spatial/body integration: parietal processing.
- Planning, choice and voluntary response: frontal systems.
- Speech production: treated as an integrated auditory + somatosensory + motor process rather than a single isolated speech center.

## Prediction pipeline

Vedic evidence → testimony cluster → private terminology → sensory entry → recognition → decision → speech/motor response → prose.

The private terminology still supplies the concrete nouns and event forms. The neuroscience-inspired layer determines how those concrete elements are arranged into a plausible human sequence.
